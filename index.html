About the Event

Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 is a collaborative event organized at Vignan's Nirula Institute of Technology and Science for Women, bringing together students, educators and industry experts to explore the world of Quantum Computing using IBM Qiskit.

Through expert sessions, hands-on workshops and an exciting hackathon, participants will learn, build and innovate with real-world quantum technologies.
